The Story of Hajari

Five years of refined taste, global reach, rooted in Palestine.

Since our start in Palestine five years ago, Hajari has grown into an international materials atelier, curating marble, limestone, and heritage Jerusalem stone with design sensitivity and sourcing discipline.

Our Positioning

We do not sell stone as inventory. We present it as a design decision.

That difference changes everything: the pace of discovery, the clarity of recommendations, the quality of visual presentation, and the trust clients feel while choosing surfaces that define how a project will be remembered.

From refined marble statements to limestone palettes and Jerusalem stone for heritage-rich architecture, Hajari is built to support projects that need beauty, confidence, and execution in the same place.
